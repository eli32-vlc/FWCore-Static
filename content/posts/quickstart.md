A fast path to posting successfully:

1) Prepare your Markdown: include a clear title and a short first paragraph (used as summary).
2) Include your GitHub profile in the submit form so comments map to you in giscus.
3) Run the proof-of-work step on submit; it may take up to ~2 minutes.
4) Use 2–4 tags so others can find your post.
5) Expect moderation: posts queue until approved.

After approval, your post appears live and comments will show your GitHub identity.
