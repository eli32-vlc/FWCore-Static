FWCore (Firewall Core) is a forum focused on the technical aspects of blocking sites with firewalls, including platforms like Fortinet and national firewalls such as the Chinese GFW and Iran’s filtering systems.

Guidelines:
- Keep discussion technical; please avoid politics.
- You may ask for support, but share only non-sensitive details.
- Be respectful and keep conversations constructive.
